This is the ScanTrix AI connector. It lets Claude and other AI assistants manage your invoices, vendors, and QuickBooks data directly.

Connector URL

  1. 1 In Claude, go to Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector and paste the URL above.
  2. 2 Claude opens a browser — sign in with your ScanTrix email and password.
  3. 3 Once connected, ask Claude to run savetrix_get_started to set up QuickBooks and explore available tools.
